- **Gotchas and CLAUDE.md rules are scope boundaries, read literally** — satisfy them and stop there. A gotcha about one module is not licence to refactor it.
- **The hooks verify** (tsc, INDEX, forbidden patterns). No extra self-review pass on top of them.
- **task-log entries: substance only** — no filler sections, no restating the diff.
- Two readings of a rule that lead to materially different work → ask before writing code.
